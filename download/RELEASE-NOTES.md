**RipScout BBS 2.6.0: Two-factor sign-in at signup**

- **New members are offered two-factor sign-in** as the last step of signup. It shows the QR code to scan with Google Authenticator (or any authenticator app), with **Skip for now**. Members who skip can turn it on any time in Your Settings > Two-Factor.
- **You decide how strict the board is:** Sysop Admin > Board Settings > **Two-factor sign-in**, or the Control Panel's Settings tab. Changes are live.
  - **Optional** (the default): offered at signup, can be skipped.
  - **Required for sysops**: sysop accounts must have it.
  - **Required for everyone**: every member must have it.
  - Anyone required to use it who hasn't set it up is walked through setup right after logging in. If they leave without finishing, they're told why and logged off.
- The authenticator name setting moved onto the same screen (**Change Name**).

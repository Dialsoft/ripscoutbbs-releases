**RipScout BBS 2.7.1: Input boxes work the way you'd expect**

- **Every screen with more than one box** (Login, Change Password, Email, posting a message, Your Details) now works like a normal Windows dialog:
  - **Tab** or **Down arrow** goes to the next box; **Shift+Tab** or **Up arrow** goes back.
  - **Click a box** to put the cursor in it.
  - What you've typed stays in each box while you move around.
  - **Enter** moves to the next box, or submits when you're on the last box or everything is filled in.
  - The screen's main button (**Login**, **Save**...) submits from anywhere; **Esc** or **Cancel** cancels.
- **Signup wizard:** Shift+Tab goes back a step, like the Back button.
- Includes everything in 2.7.0: the installer, Internet Check, crisp menu lettering and the classic RIP art pack.

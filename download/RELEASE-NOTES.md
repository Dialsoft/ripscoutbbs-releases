**RipScout BBS 2.8.1: Update progress bar**

- **Update progress:** while an update is under way, a progress bar runs across the top of the Control Panel (on every tab):
  - **Downloading** with the percentage and megabytes,
  - **Checking** the download against the checksum published on GitHub,
  - the **countdown** while callers are warned ("Installing version ... in 25 seconds"),
  - **Installing**, then **Restarting**. When the board comes back as the new version, the Control Panel reloads itself.

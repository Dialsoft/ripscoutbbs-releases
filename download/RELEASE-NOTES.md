**RipScout BBS 2.7.2: The Control Panel refreshes itself after an update**

- **Control Panel:** a Control Panel window left open during an update used to keep showing the old page (with the new version number on top), so new tabs, buttons and settings didn't appear until it was closed and opened again. It now reloads itself when the board comes back as a new version, and says "Update finished".
- Includes 2.7.1 (input boxes work like a normal dialog: Tab, Shift+Tab, arrows, click a box) and 2.7.0 (installer, Internet Check, crisp menu lettering, classic RIP art pack).

**RipscoutBBS 1.8.1: Hear the music**

- Most terminals, DialComm included, keep ANSI music switched off until you ask. Now the board says so:
  - a line on the welcome screen: "This board has sound! Turn on ANSI Music in your terminal to hear the tunes."
  - a one-time notification on each caller's first visit, telling DialComm users to click "Music Off" on the status line
  - a reminder on the RIP Showcase's Sound page.
- When two notifications arrive together (like "You have mail" and the music tip at login), they now take turns instead of the second one replacing the first.
- Fixed: after a notification that played a tune, the screen underneath could come back with a stray copy of the title.

If you're running 1.8.0, this update installs itself the next time nobody is online.

**RipscoutBBS 1.2.0: BMX Newsmap and QR codes**

- **BMX Newsmap** on the main menu: current BMX news from RSS feeds, drawn as a RIPscrip treemap in the style of newsmap.jp. Bigger tiles are newer stories, colors are categories (Racing, Freestyle, Bikes & Gear, Events & Video), and bright tiles are from today. Click a tile to read the story.
- **QR codes in RIPscrip:** each story shows a QR code, encoded by the BBS and drawn with RIP bars. Scan it with your phone to open the full article.
- **Feed list you can edit:** Sysop Admin > News & Rules > BMX News Feeds (one RSS/Atom link per line, takes effect live).

If you're running 1.1.0, this update installs itself the next time nobody is online.
